But climates are different and now they're changing.

In the early 1900s, entire homes could be ordered from a catalog and assembled by me and you.

Parts arrived by rail, instructions arrived on paper. Many of those homes still stand today

The future of housing may not be about building bigger or more permanent homes. It may be about building homes that repsond better to the places they exist in.
