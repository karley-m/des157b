Capstone Proposal

The topic I want to explore for my capstone project is: what will housing look like in the future if we continue on this destructive path… and what if housing could look different?

Building homes generates massive amounts of carbon, suburban sprawl continues to make us car dependent and isolated, there is a shortage of affordable housing, homelessness is at a record high, and major cities are becoming more and more susceptible to climate disasters with mass migrations already occurring.

I want to explore this topic through the lens of a dystopian narrative, and also through some data visualization. I was thinking about creating a similar interface like Zillow to slowly uncover this dystopian future through new metrics of what we consider luxury, comfort, or security in this future. For this Zillow-like interface, I imagine it being introduced through a narrative click-through interaction, being guided by an AI real estate agent. Usual perks for homes will be replaced with how well does this home resist heat or floods. Questions will arise in the user: what is now considered luxury? How has climate change reshaped our geography? How has privacy and ownership collapsed or shifted? What would it be like if my landlord was artificial intelligence? To create this click-through, I would like to use animation libraries to add effects to the text that shows up.

Possible challenges I’m imagining is how to find the right balance between showing and letting the users make their own thoughts and assumptions about this dystopian world. I would like the narrative to end on a hopeful note. This will turn a bit speculative as I’m interested in introducing or reminding the user of imaginative housing found in the solarpunk movement. I also need to do ample research on housing to back-up the narrative choices I make about this future.

My main target audience for this project would be young adults or recent college grads who may be thinking about housing in their future. Currently, the average age of a home owner in this country is 40 - 50 years old. This interaction would be as if the user has finally reached the home owner age in the year 2060 or 2070.
