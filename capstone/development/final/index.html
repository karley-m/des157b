For most of human history, people lived closer to the environments around them.

We moved with
seasons,
water,
wind,
shade,
and weather.

Shelter responded to climate because it had to.

Over time, we built more permanent systems. Homes became standardized, and cheap energy made it easier to build the same way almost anywhere.

But climates are different and now they're changing.

In the early 1900s, entire homes could be ordered from a catalog and assembled by an ordinary person.

Parts arrived by rail and instructions arrived on paper. Many of those homes still stand today.

The future of housing may not be about building bigger or more permanent homes. It may be about building homes that repsond better to the places they exist in.

Build a home kit designed for its environment with alternative, sustainable parts.
