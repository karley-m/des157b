Usability Testing & Results

Observations:

I noticed the people testing my project enjoyed the visuals and the stylization of them.

I noticed on the home assembling screen that some of the users wanted certain items to be behind others, but there were no layering options available for them to customize to a higher degree.

Some users took their time scrolling through the introductory pages, so I'm considering shortening the amount of text or condensing the scrollable frames to reduce the amount they have to go through. Also thinking about adding some accompanying visuals or imagery to give context to the kit idea.

Things to address from the results:

My main focus for improvements is the introductory pages. While there are some functionality issues, I want to connect the visuals of this page to the style I have in the rest of the project, which is the collage/mixed-media visuals.

Next, I want to address the home builder canvas and shuffle around the UI on that page for desktop view, putting the canvas to the left, with the buttons to the right so the user doesn't have to scroll.

Users were able to figure out how to navigate and interact with my project and mentioned that it was intuitive. Some things missing from making it more intituive would be an arrow to prompt them to start scrolling on the very first page, and a progress bar for the steps.

I think overall the users were prompted to think critically about sustainable design in homes. One user requested that the effect of their choices be visible in the neighborhood section to compare and contrast their choices, which I think would help strengthen my idea.

To strengthen my visual cohesion, I am planning on changing the background based on the climate the user selects, creating more steps for the 'Save Design' button that builds anticipation, creating a legend for understanding the compatibility dots in the Tooltips, and possibly animating some of the options on hover for excitement.

My plan is to start by fixing the functionality, adjust the UI and content of the intro screens and the canvas screen, and then tackle the point system and add a progress bar within the steps.
